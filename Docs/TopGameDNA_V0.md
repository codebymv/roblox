## 1. Per-game skeletons

Each entry is read the same way: what is the session, what survives the session, what makes it
social, what makes it a clip, and what makes someone open it again tomorrow.

### Grow a Garden 2 (~621K CCU, 8-player servers, ~59 min sessions)
- Session: open-ended tending. No round boundary at all.
- Survives session: the farm itself, which grows **while you are offline**.
- Social: circular map with every farm facing a shared central hub, deliberately manufacturing
  foot traffic past your crops. Guilds with weekly rewards. Night-time stealing phase.
- Clip: rare mutation harvest, or getting raided.
- Return reason: crops finished growing, shop restocked, weekly guild reset.

### Murder Mystery 2 (peak 1.1M during Summer 2026 event, 12-player rounds)
- Session: 2-4 minute rounds, three roles, randomized each round.
- Survives session: XP level and a **tradeable knife inventory**. Hundreds of godlies; seasonal
  boxes revalue old inventories. Lapsed players log in to trade without playing rounds.
- Social: the trade economy *is* the metagame. 12 players is tuned so the murderer has cover
  but every player still matters.
- Clip: the last-second sheriff shot.
- Return reason: seasonal events, knife value shifts.

### 99 Nights in the Forest (~288K CCU, 25-player servers)
- Session: co-op camp survival across a day/night cycle.
- Survives session: unlocks, and the day counter itself as a ladder toward 99.
- Social: 25 players around a shared camp; rescuing missing children *shortens* the ladder,
  so cooperation is mechanically compensated.
- Clip: the thing in the trees reaching the campfire.
- Return reason: weekly Update Parties (Saturdays), two-week content rhythm, and limited biomes
  with disposable event currencies (Bee Mini Biome / Honey) that cost little to build.

### Animal Hospital (Anomaly) (30-player servers, parties of 4)
- **Our closest structural comp.** Session: a "Shift" - a bounded co-op round with role splits.
- Survives session: Animal Coins buy **permanent class unlocks** (Intern free, Nurse 10, Secretary
  120, Paramedic 250, Psychologist 500, Doctor 900, Security 1250, Surgeon 2500). Plus a Journal
  that tracks discovered anomalies.
- Ladder: shifts are endless and escalate. Shift 1 is a tutorial, 2 adds photo checks, 4 adds
  Ambulance events, 5+ stacks everything. There is no ending.
- Social: 30-player lobby, parties of four, total shifts displayed in the lobby as status.
- Clip: a Skinwalker you waved through.
- Return reason: next class unlock, deeper shift number.

### Blox Fruits (long-tail RPG)
- Session: open-world grind, no round.
- Survives session: level, fruits, gear - all of it.
- Social: raids, trading, PvP.
- Return reason: level ceiling raised by updates; RPG retention benchmarks are the platform's best
  (roughly 35% D1 / 16% D7 / 7.8% D30).

### Anime Expeditions (unit collection)
- Session: expedition runs.
- Survives session: gacha unit roster.
- Return reason: banner rotation. Content treadmill is the entire model.

### +1 Speed Keyboard Escape (~127-455K peak, ~8 min sessions)
- Session: 8 minutes. Deliberately tiny.
- Survives session: rebirth multipliers (x1.5 at rebirth 1, escalating to x100B at rebirth 20),
  Trails and Auras bought with Wins.
- The whole game is **one number going up**, with a reset-for-multiplier loop layered on it.
- Social: shared servers, visible speed racing.
- Return reason: Saturday Admin Abuse events, daily treadmill, exponential curve pull.

### RIVALS (322K peak, 15-30 min matches)
- Session: long, structured, plant/defuse rounds with a buy phase.
- Survives session: ranked tier (which can go *down*) and a battle pass.
- Social: team play, ranked ladder, leave penalties.
- Return reason: rank anxiety plus pass progress. Note this is the only top-10 entry with a
  meaningful punishment for quitting.

### Pet Simulator 99 (~156K CCU)
- Session: hatch, farm, rebirth.
- Survives session: pets, rebirth rank, exclusive shop access.
- Social: a dedicated 50-player **Trading Plaza** with auction boards and search terminals -
  a whole separate place whose only purpose is social density around owned items.
- Clip: a Huge pet hatch.
- Return reason: trade value shifts, event pets.

### Steal a Brainrot (~72B lifetime visits, 1.5M 24-hour peaks)
- Session: continuous PvP theft in a shared space.
- Survives session: your stolen collection and its income rate.
- Social: theft is the entire social layer - other players are the content.
- Return reason: Saturday updates and Admin Abuse events spike concurrency hard, then it settles.

## 2. The twelve traits, and our status

1. **Persistent state above the session.** All ten have it. We have none. Our endcard prints a
   number and the number ceases to exist.
2. **A single compounding visible number.** Speed, sheckles, shifts, level, rank, rebirth
   multiplier. We have no number that belongs to the player.
3. **Escalating ladder, not a binary win.** Animal Hospital shifts and 99 Nights days are endless
   and scale. Our run is "delivered: yes/no" - one bit of information per eight minutes.
4. **Lobby is a shop and a status board.** Animal Hospital's lobby class shop, Pet Sim's Trading
   Plaza. Ours is an empty waiting room with a Ready button.
5. **Social density and manufactured foot traffic.** 8 to 30 players per server, with map layouts
   that force you past other people's stuff. We put one crew alone in an empty server.
6. **Rarity variance as a jackpot.** Mutations, godlies, Huge pets. Our cargo is a generic orange
   crate and every failure is drawn from a flat deck.
7. **Push-your-luck against something owned.** Night stealing, extraction greed. Our cargo loss is
   abstract - nobody owns the cargo, so nobody grieves it.
8. **Earnable asymmetric classes that double as ethical monetization.** Animal Hospital sells
   convenience and identity, not wins. Our roles are auto-assigned, free, and identical forever.
9. **Time-to-action under ten seconds.** Roblox's own guidance is instant action inside 10s, first
   reward inside 60s, and most abandonment happens in the first two minutes. We open on a ready-up
   screen and the first failure may not fire for a minute.
10. **A live-ops rhythm.** Weekly Update Parties, Saturday events, milestone codes, disposable
    event currencies. We have no hook to hang an event on.
11. **Solo-viable, co-op superior.** Animal Hospital and 99 Nights both work alone. Our solo mode
    is a Driver with nobody to talk to.
12. **Downtime is never dead.** MM2 ghosts watch the round finish. Our failed crew stares at a
    lobby.

Note on why this matters beyond design taste: Roblox's June 2026 Recommended For You change moved
from a 7-day window to a 28-day window, scoring Day 1, Day 2-7, and Day 8-28 separately, and split
play-through rate from session quality and spend. A game with no day-2 reason to return is now
structurally throttled in discovery regardless of how good the minute-to-minute feel is.

## 3. What we synthesize (not copy)

Hauling is already a push-your-luck container. One structural change converts our binary round into
a ladder, a streak, a rarity surface, and the strongest clip in the genre simultaneously.

- **Legs instead of a run.** A convoy is leg 1, 2, 3... Each delivered leg banks nothing yet; it
  raises the multiplier and the pressure. This is the Animal Hospital shift ladder rewritten as
  distance instead of nights.
- **Bank or push.** After each delivery the crew votes for twelve seconds. Bank pays out and ends
  the convoy. Push doubles down on a harder leg. Wiping forfeits the entire unbanked stack. This is
  where the argument, and therefore the clip, lives.
- **Manifest rarity.** Cargo is rolled per leg with a rarity, a value multiplier, and one
  mechanical quirk (Fragile lowers the safe corner speed, Volatile amplifies integrity damage,
  Livestock injects panic events, Prototype is the jackpot). Data-driven, near-zero content cost,
  and it feeds a Journal.
- **Depot with four bays.** The server is a shared depot; up to four crews run parallel lanes off
  the same hub. You walk past other crews' rigs and see their leg number and carried value on the
  board. Grow a Garden's foot-traffic trick, expressed as a freight yard.
- **Freight Credits and role kits.** Banked hauls pay credits; credits buy permanent per-role kits
  that tilt timing windows and never bypass the loop. Animal Hospital's class economy, kept
  strictly non-pay-to-win.
- **Convoy streak.** Consecutive banked convoys, shown on your nameplate and the depot board. This
  is our one compounding number.

## 4. Patterns we are deliberately declining

Listed so future decisions do not quietly drift into them.

- **Player-to-player trading economies.** Enormous retention lift, and the single largest source of
  scamming and off-platform value markets targeting minors. Not worth it.
- **Gacha and egg-hatch RNG as the primary progression.** We use rarity for cargo *variance inside a
  run*, not as a paid pull.
- **AFK treadmill farming.** +1 Speed's treadmill and Grow a Garden's offline growth work for their
  genres, but rewarding an empty chair is the opposite of a co-op crew game.
- **PvP theft of persistent property.** Steal a Brainrot's engine. Our risk is self-inflicted greed,
  not another player ruining your evening.
- **Ranked leave penalties.** RIVALS can afford them at 322K CCU. A prototype cannot.
- **Paid power.** Robux items stay cosmetic. Every kit that touches a timing window is earnable with
  in-game credits.

## 5. Metric implications

Adds to `ValidationMetrics_V0.md`:

- D1 / D7 return rate (platform benchmark: D1 20% good, 30% great, 40% top-tier).
- Average legs per convoy, and the distribution - a healthy curve peaks at 2-4, not 1.
- Bank-vs-push ratio. If push is under ~35% the reward curve is too flat; over ~75% and the loss is
  not landing.
- Credits earned per session, and time-to-first-unlock (target: first kit affordable inside
  two or three sessions).
- Time from spawn to first drive input (target under 10 seconds).
